## Core Technical Areas Covered

### 1. Water Treatment Plant (WTP) & Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) Operations
* **Preliminary & Primary Treatment:** Raw water intake screening, grit and coarse screen management, inlet chambers, and primary sedimentation clarifiers designed to remove suspended solids, oils, and grease.
* **Secondary Biological Treatment:** Activated sludge processes utilizing aeration tanks, air blowers, anoxic chambers for nitrogen denitrification, and secondary clarifiers to reduce Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD).
* **Tertiary & Advanced Treatment:** Gravity sand filters, UV-C disinfection chambers, chlorination contact chambers, and activated carbon odor control systems.
* **Sludge Handling:** Solid bowl dewatering centrifuges, sludge thickeners, and return sludge pumping systems operating at targeted return ratios.

### 2. Hydraulic & Process Design Calculations
* **Head Loss Analysis:** Accounting for frictional losses via Darcy-Weisbach equations alongside minor losses from 90-degree elbows, 45-degree elbows, structural entrances, and square exits under peak and average flow conditions.
* **Weir Hydraulics:** Mathematical modeling of rectangular and V-notch weir discharge equations to determine head levels over weirs from treatment basins to launders.
* **Pipe Sizing:** Dimensioning pipelines across gravity-fed conduits (velocity range: 0.4 to 0.9 m/s), pressurized pump lines (velocity range: 1.0 to 2.0 m/s), and air blower stainless steel headers (velocity range: 5.0 to 20.0 m/s) using materials such as UPVC, HDPE, and Stainless Steel.

### 3. Project Management via Oracle Primavera P6
* **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS):** Structuring complex multi-month infrastructure projects—such as the construction of a 3,000 cubic meter Glass-Fused-Steel (GFS) tank and replacement of existing fiberglass tanks in Mina.
* **Scheduling & Critical Path Method (CPM):** Managing activity dependencies, calculating total float, tracking milestone achievements, and evaluating data dates across four distinct project phases (Engineering/Design, Procurement, Execution, and Handover).
* **Earned Value Management (EVM):** Monitoring project health through Planned Value (PV), Earned Value (EV), Actual Total Cost (ATC), Cost Performance Index (CPI), and Schedule Performance Index (SPI) metrics.
* **Resource Allocation:** Loading labor, equipment, and material resources with assigned unit pricing to optimize cost performance and prevent overallocation.
